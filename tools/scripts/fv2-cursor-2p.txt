# Fighting Vipers 2: moving player 2's cursor in TRAINING, to see which byte follows it.
#
# In training one pair of hands chooses both fighters: player 1's punch confirms his, and from there his
# directions are routed to player 2's cursor (console_play::route_training). So a punch and then some
# rights move the 2P cursor.
#
#   1300  Start on the title screen -> the list, where VF3_CONSOLE_MODE takes TRAINING
#   VF3_CONSOLE_MODE=training VF3_RAM_WATCH=00279660:16 VF3_KEYS="F12@1650,F12@1760,F12@1860"
#
# 0x00279668 is player 1's cursor, read at 0x00060458 (docs/fv2-select-names.md); 0x00279669 is read
# right beside it everywhere, and that is the hypothesis this route tries.
1300 press p1.start 8
1600 press p1.punch 8
1700 press p1.right 6
1740 press p1.right 6
1780 press p1.right 6
1820 press p1.right 6
